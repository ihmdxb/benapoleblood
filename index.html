Benefits of Blood Donation

Heart Health: Regular blood donation helps maintain iron balance, reducing the risk of heart disease.

New Cell Generation: After donation, the body replenishes blood with fresh new cells, keeping you healthy.

Calorie Burn: Donating one pint of blood can burn up to 650 calories.

Mental Peace: The joy of saving a human life provides immense mental satisfaction.

Safety & privacy

Your number is shared only with people who search for donors or open a request. Switch off "Available to Donate" in your profile to stop appearing in donor search.

Meet at a hospital or blood bank. Never donate or pay at a private location, and never send money to someone you have not verified.

Blood must not be sold. BenapoleBlood is a free volunteer platform. Report any request that asks for payment to the admin team.

Emergency? Call 999 first, then post a request here.
